OUR INITIATIVES

How We Make a Difference

Our projects focus on practical support, community welfare and human dignity.

Ramzan Ration Drive

Essential grocery packages are distributed to deserving families to help them manage household food requirements during Ramadan.

Iftar Boxes

Prepared Iftar boxes are distributed to people who need assistance during the holy month.

Monthly Food Drive

Monthly meal distribution initiatives provide freshly prepared food, including biryani, to communities.

Madrasa Project

Educational support includes reading tables, Quran Pak, air coolers, heaters and sweaters for students.

Roadside Drive of Hope

This initiative reaches roadside individuals with food and humanitarian support.

Atta Drive

Flour distribution helps families fulfil a basic household food need.
