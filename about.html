OUR STORY

Built on the belief that every person matters.

Dilnawaz Foundation was established with the aim of serving people and contributing to the wellbeing of communities.

Over the years, the foundation has taken part in community-focused activities and welfare initiatives designed to provide practical support where it is needed.

Our journey continues through the efforts of volunteers, supporters and community members who believe that positive change begins with collective action.

WHAT DRIVES US

Our Mission & Vision

Our Mission

To support individuals and communities through compassionate, transparent and practical welfare initiatives.

Our Vision

To contribute towards communities where people have access to support, opportunity and hope, especially during times of need.

Our Values

Compassion, dignity, integrity, transparency, community participation and responsible service.
